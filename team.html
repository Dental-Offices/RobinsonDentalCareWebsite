Meet Our Doctors

Our team of experienced, compassionate dentists is dedicated to providing personalized care that puts your comfort and confidence first.

General Dentist • DMD, MS

Dr. Meer Ahmed

Dr. Meer Ahmed has been in dentistry for the past 15 years. A friendly and dedicated general dentist in Moon Township, PA, Dr. Ahmed enjoys getting to know his patients and takes his passion for dental care and his patients' smiles to heart. He believes in a personalized approach to every patient, establishing a genuine connection and providing excellent individualized care.

Before working as a dentist in Moon Township, Dr. Ahmed completed his education in India and pursued higher education from the Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons of Glasgow, UK. He worked and honed his clinical skills in renowned hospitals in Middle Eastern countries. To advance his career, Dr. Ahmed immigrated to the U.S. to study at the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine, where he completed his Masters and Doctorate degree with many awards and honors.

Dr. Ahmed makes every effort to stay on top of current developments in dentistry through continuous education and active membership in several international organizations. His scope of dental expertise encompasses surgical extractions, root canals, crown and bridge treatments, implants, Invisalign, and removable dentures.

Dr. Ahmed believes he can make a difference in patients' lives by actively involving them in their treatment decision process and educating them on how to maintain a healthy smile — and a healthy body. When not working, Dr. Ahmed loves to come up with new recipes in the kitchen, travel with his family, and cuddle with his kids.

American Dental Association (ADA) Pennsylvania Dental Association (PDA) Royal College of Physicians & Surgeons of Glasgow University of Pittsburgh DMD & MS

General Dentist • DMD

Dr. Lutfiie Abliaieva

Dr. Lutfiie has been passionate about helping people achieve healthy, confident smiles since beginning her dental career in 2012. She earned her first dental degree in Crimea, Ukraine, where she spent three years practicing as a general dentist in an underserved community. Those early experiences taught her the importance of listening to patients, building trust, and providing compassionate, high-quality care to everyone who walks through the door.

After moving to the United States, Dr. Abliaieva fulfilled her dream of continuing her dental career by earning her Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) degree from the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine. She combines her international experience with the latest evidence-based techniques to provide personalized care for patients of all ages.

Dr. Abliaieva believes that going to the dentist should be a positive and comfortable experience. She takes the time to get to know each patient, answer questions, and explain treatment options so they can feel informed and confident about their oral health. Whether it's a routine checkup, restoring a smile, or helping someone overcome dental anxiety, her goal is to make every visit as stress-free and rewarding as possible.

When she's not in the office, Dr. Abliaieva enjoys spending time with family and friends, exploring Pittsburgh's parks and neighborhoods, and discovering new places to travel. She looks forward to welcoming you to the practice and helping you smile with confidence!

American Dental Association Pennsylvania Academy of General Dentistry University of Pittsburgh DMD
