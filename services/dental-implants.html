Dental Implants

Crowns and conventional bridges or dentures may not be your only options when replacing missing teeth. Dental implants offer a smile that looks and feels very natural.

Request an Appointment

Tooth Implants

When should I consider one?

You should consider a tooth implant if you are missing one or more teeth.

What are dental implants?

Implants are among the most realistic tooth replacements available. A post is surgically anchored into the jawbone, allowed to heal for several months, and then topped with an artificial tooth that closely mimics a natural tooth in both appearance and function.

Alternatives

A bridge or partial denture may be considered if the financial burden of implants is too great.

Implant Dentures

When should I consider them?

Implant dentures can help if your current dentures are loose, uncomfortable, or difficult to chew with.

What are implant dentures?

Usually two to four implants are placed in the bone and allowed to heal. After healing, a removable denture with special attachments clips onto a bar connected to the implants, holding the denture firmly in place while speaking and chewing.

Implant retained dentures are mainly supported by the gums and bone, while implant supported dentures are designed for patients who need more structural support from the implants themselves.

Alternatives

New conventional dentures may be considered when implants are not the right financial fit.
